# Suggestions

An assistant proposes an action from what is said in a channel: after « ok on parle lundi », « You are free on Monday from 9:30 to 9:45, schedule the meeting? », to the person who wrote it and to the person who wrote the message before it, each with their own assistant. The harness reads nothing of private exchanges, not even their metadata, but an encrypted room (a conversation or a channel) where one of its members brought their own assistant in and every other person said yes (below): otherwise it sees only the rooms, neither encrypted nor direct, inside a space or not, that a visible user of its own, the channel listener, was invited to. No assistant joins any channel. Suggestions are off unless `SUGGESTIONS_ENABLED=true`: off, nothing below runs, and the listener declines its invites and leaves the rooms it is still in, at each start of the matrix role.

- **The listener.** One Matrix user in the application service's exclusive users namespace, `@<SUGGESTIONS_USER_LOCALPART>:<MATRIX_SERVER_NAME>` (`@twake-assistant:<server>` by default), registered at the start of the matrix role, when suggestions are on, and named « Twake Assistant » (« Assistant Twake » with `ASSISTANT_LOCALE=fr`). Synapse pushes the rooms it is a member of, and no other: the registration has no room namespace. Room admins invite it from Twake Chat, and a member list shows it. It never sends a message, in a channel or anywhere.
- **Which rooms it accepts.** On an invite it declines a direct room (`is_direct`) and a room whose stripped state shows encryption, without joining. Otherwise it joins and reads the room's state (as `roomEncryption()` would for an assistant, not through its crypto: the listener has no device): it stays, and reads the room for as long as it is a member, unless the room has an `m.room.encryption` (the stripped state may leave it out); else it leaves with the reason. It checks the same again for each room it is in when the matrix role starts. When a room it is in turns encrypted (an `m.room.encryption` state event, or an encrypted event), it leaves at once. Every room that is not encrypted, inside a space or not, is read continuously once the listener is in it; it never analyses an encrypted room nor a direct room: private exchanges are only seen when their user sends a message on purpose (« Ask my assistant »), or brings their own assistant in, as the next point says.
- **An encrypted room, by its owner's choice and everyone's yes.** The listener never enters one. Its member may invite their own assistant into it, as Twake Chat offers after a warning (D128 there): invited by its owner into an encrypted room, a direct conversation or a channel, once every other person in it, joined or invited, accepted (Twake Chat's `app.twake.chat.assistant_request` of the owner and `app.twake.chat.assistant_consent` of each other person, each keyed by its sender with the assistant in `assistant_id`, the yes given after the request), the assistant joins and stays, greets no one, never takes a turn nor writes there, and reads what everyone writes, for suggestions to its owner alone (`assistant_listened_rooms`). Chat shares no key of the history, so it reads what is sent from then on. Without that yes it leaves at once, without a word. It stops, and the room is forgotten, once it leaves or a member removes it; a newcomer who has not said yes, its owner or the last other person leaving, a withdrawn request or a no makes it leave without a word. The checks of a room run one at a time and a message of the room waits for those registered before it, so what a newcomer writes as they come in is never read. Other assistants, the listener and bots ask no yes: a bot is, as in Twake Chat, a member of our homeserver whose id starts with `@bot_` or `@hermes_` (on another server anyone chooses their localpart, so such a member is a person and is asked), or who announces its commands in the room (`org.matrix.msc4332.commands` or `m.bot.commands`, under its own state key). The invite of a bot already triggers the check, so an announcement helps only a bot that announced before the assistant came. A bot is never the person suggestions go to, and a room with bots and no person makes the assistant leave without a word. A room may hold the assistants of several members (`assistant_listened_rooms` keyed by room and assistant), each reading for its owner alone, and a person answers each assistant separately: the consent is one state event keyed by its sender, `{"answers": {"<assistant user id>": {"accepted": true, "ts": <ms>}}}`, so a yes to one assistant never withdraws another. The older content `{assistant_id, accepted, ts}` is still read, as the single answer it is, when `answers` is not an object. An answer counts only if its time, the earlier of its `ts` and the event's server time (the server time alone for the older content), is after the request. A person who said yes, left and was invited again is asked again: a yes counts only if given after their latest join or invite. A check of the room that cannot complete makes the assistant leave. It needs migration 0077 (`assistant_listened_rooms` keyed by room and assistant) run with the new matrix image first; rolling the image back means undoing 0077.
- **The switch of a channel.** Kicking or banning the listener, or removing it from the room, stops the analysis of that room at once: this is the switch room admins use from Twake Chat. Nothing else is needed, and nothing of the room is kept.
- **The pre-filter.** A pure function (`src/suggestions/prefilter.ts`, French and English) runs on each clear message before any admission or request to Synapse: the message must name a date or time (a weekday, tomorrow, next week, 10h, 3pm, 12/10, 3 novembre) and show the intent to meet (on parle, on se voit, se voir, réunion, call, rdv, catch up, let's talk), and be 600 characters or less. Only then does it go further.
- **The decision model.** With `LLM_DECISION_MODEL` set, a decision model such as `nox-4b` tells the messages that arrange a meeting in place of the words: it reads the message and the one before it, each author as a letter, and answers with a probability; at 0.8 or more the suggestion goes on. It reads a message only once someone may be offered something from it: someone who wrote it or the one before has an assistant, has not turned suggestions off and has not muted the room; and never the message of whoever turned suggestions off. The matrix role calls it with its own client, at most 256 tokens within 5 seconds, eight sortings at once: past that, or whenever a sorting fails or answers no probability, the words decide (`channel sorted by the words`, `channel not sorted`). Each sorting logs `channel sorted` with its probability and duration, never the message.
- **Context, in memory.** The matrix role holds the last message of each room (and, for an encrypted room, of each owner reading it) in its memory only, as context for the next, for fifteen minutes: then it goes within the minute, whether a next message came or not. A model is handed the message that matched and the one before it, at most. The only place a quote is written is the payload of the `suggest` job, which carries it to the api role and is deleted when the model has answered or the job is dropped (a job over ten minutes old, or one that finds suggestions turned off, is dropped without being read; the handler never fails, so a failed job never keeps its quotes), or with the owner's assistant (see Consent). A job that waits for its owner to let their assistant read their calendar keeps them no longer: ten minutes from the message read at most (see The model).
- **Who is offered something.** The author of the message and the author of the one before it, when they differ, each only if they have an assistant, have not turned suggestions off, have not muted the room, and are under their caps: three suggestions in a rolling day and one per room in twelve hours. Whoever turned suggestions off is never quoted: if the message that matched is theirs nothing happens, and their message is not quoted for the other; like any message of the channel, it is only held in memory as context (above). A suggestion needs the other person: a message alone, with no message before it, proposes nothing and asks nothing.
- **The model.** The job runs in the api role, outside any session: a fixed system prompt, the quotes as one fenced block marked as untrusted data with the author's address and the room, and only the contracts `find_meeting_slots` and `create_meeting`. When the owner never allowed an application that the suggestion reads, here their calendar, the job asks the first-use question in their assistant's room, in their language, saying that it is to propose a slot for a message of the other person, by address and with no quote, and waits for the answer, ten minutes from the message at most: a yes lets it go on, and a no, a newer question in that room or no answer by then drops it, quotes included. It asks once per application and level: not while a request for that permission waits, its own or one of the owner's turns, which it then waits for, and never again after a no, but again, for a later message, once its question expired or a newer request superseded it. The call frozen for that question holds nothing of the messages, and the owner's yes to it ends their turn on the harness's thanks: no model runs it, and no direct tool call can. The contracts decide what is asked: a write never is, since the meeting always waits for the owner. The code holds both calls to the messages, whatever the model writes: `find_meeting_slots` looks only at the calendars of the owner and of the people who wrote the messages, the attendees of `create_meeting` can only be those people, by their address, never one a message names, a declined slot is refused, and the meeting keeps its title, slot, zone and attendees alone, no description or place, so that the owner approves all of what they are shown. The turn's messages are dropped after it: nothing goes to history or memory. The model's call to `create_meeting` freezes as a pending call of origin `suggestion`, which waits for its owner whatever they allowed (it is a high-risk write), like the write of an event's turn; if the model prepares no meeting, nothing happens. The turn the owner's yes resumes in their assistant's room is still the suggestion's: it is offered these two contracts alone, held to the owner and the people the meeting invites, makes four calls at most besides that meeting, as a suggestion does, fewer when `TURN_MAX_TOOL_CALLS` is lower, and reads none of the owner's memory or skills; another meeting it prepares waits for them again. Nothing is ever posted in the channel.
- **Where the owner sees it.** In Twake Space, if `SPACE_API_URL` is set: `POST {SPACE_API_URL}/notifications/suggestions` with `Authorization: Bearer {SPACE_API_TOKEN}` and `{ "matrixUserId", "externalId", "text", "pendingCallId", "matrixRoomId" }`, where `externalId` and `pendingCallId` are the pending call's id, `text` the sentence the harness writes from the call (day, hours, title as plain text, the people invited, at most 500 characters, never the model's own words) and `matrixRoomId` the channel. A 201 or a 200 is done; a 200 with a null `id` means the user turned suggestions off in Space; a 404 (`unknown_user`) or a 403 is logged with its status and nothing else. Space's buttons call `POST /v1/pending-calls/{id}/approve` and `/refuse` with the user's token, whose audience may be one of `AUTH_ANSWER_AUDIENCES`, accepted on these two routes alone. The proposal is also written in the owner's assistant room, as an event's turn is, with the usual yes or no. Without `SPACE_API_URL` only the room has it.
- **When it fails.** A message that passed the pre-filter and got no proposal because the model or the job failed, or because the calendar did not answer (the call never reached it, or it failed on its side with a 5xx; a 4xx is its answer and stays silent, and so does a calendar that answered again after a failed search), is followed by a notice to its owner in their assistant's room, once per message, with no word of the message and nothing in Space. In a listened conversation a model failure is tried again every few seconds within the ten minutes, and the notice comes with the last failure. A message the model reads as no meeting, a full calendar, a cap or a mute stay silent.
- **Refusing.** `POST /v1/pending-calls/{id}/refuse` takes an optional body `{ "reason": "another_time" | "not_useful" }`. For a suggestion, `another_time` queues one new suggestion that excludes the slot refused, from the title and the attendees of the refused call (no message is kept to read again); a suggestion that was itself a second try is not tried a third time. `not_useful` mutes that room for that user for seven days, even when the suggestion was answered already, expired or replaced, which still gets a 409. A refusal without a reason, or of any other call, changes nothing more than it did.
- **Turning it off.** The organization, with `SUGGESTIONS_ENABLED`; a channel, for everyone, by removing the listener (above); a user, with `PUT /v1/suggestions/settings` `{ "enabled": false, "mutedRooms": [] }` (or their provisioner for them, see Provisioning API), which turns off both reading their messages and offering them anything, and a member can mute a room for themselves. `GET` shows the same: `mutedRooms` lists the rooms muted now, for good or after a refusal, and `PUT` replaces those muted for good.
- **What is kept.** A suggestion keeps its channel, its slot and whether it was a second try, never a message: for a day, which the caps count, or the lifetime of a request when longer, and as long as its call waits for the owner. The worker role's hourly pass forgets the others, and the mutes that ended. Deleting an assistant erases its owner's suggestions and those still to make, forgets the encrypted conversations it read for them, and keeps their switch and the rooms they muted, which are theirs and not their assistant's.
- **Logs.** The suggestion path logs identifiers, counts and outcomes at `info` (`suggestions queued`, `suggestion skipped`, `suggestion asks for consent`, `suggestion made`, `suggestion decided`, `suggestion failure notice queued`, `suggestion sent to Space`, `suggestions switched`), never a message. A suggestion's turn logs nothing below `info`, whatever `LOG_LEVEL`: the prompt, which holds the quotes, and the arguments the model writes from them, which a turn logs at `debug`, reach no log. Nothing is used to train a model.
